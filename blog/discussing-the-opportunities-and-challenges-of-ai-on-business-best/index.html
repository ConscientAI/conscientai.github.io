Discussing the opportunities and challenges of AI on Business Best

Nov 25, 2025 · 3 min read

We were excited to be featured on Business Best, a business interview program on Ada Derana 24. The conversation provided an opportunity to share our journey in artificial intelligence, discuss how the AI landscape is evolving and explore what it takes to build meaningful AI solutions from Sri Lanka for a global market.

Our co-founder, C.D. Athuraliya, spoke about how a long-standing interest in artificial intelligence led to the creation of Sri Lanka’s first machine learning meetup in 2015 and eventually to the founding of ConscientAI. The discussion highlighted the importance of building strong communities, creating opportunities for collaboration and fostering a research-driven culture that enables AI innovation to grow sustainably.

One of the key themes discussed during the interview was the relationship between cutting-edge AI research and real-world business applications. Rather than viewing research as separate from commercial work, we shared our perspective that organizations working in AI need to stay closely connected to advances in research while taking a pragmatic approach to solving business problems. This balance has shaped our work with organizations across North America, Europe, Australia, Japan and Sri Lanka, where we focus on designing bespoke AI solutions that create measurable value rather than applying AI for its own sake.

The conversation also explored the rapid evolution of AI and what it means for businesses and society. While AI is transforming the way organizations operate, we emphasized that it is not a silver bullet capable of solving every problem. Successful AI adoption requires a clear understanding of both the opportunities and the limitations of the technology, supported by thoughtful strategy rather than hype. We also discussed the future of work, highlighting the importance of developing uniquely human skills alongside technical expertise, while ensuring that the next generation of professionals continues to have opportunities to learn and grow.

Another important topic was the role of Sri Lanka in the global AI ecosystem. We believe that meaningful AI innovation should not be confined to a handful of countries. Building AI that truly serves different cultures and languages requires broader participation, making diversity, inclusion and local research increasingly important. Through our continued investment in collaborative research, international publications and practical AI development, we hope to contribute to a stronger AI ecosystem both in Sri Lanka and beyond.

We would like to thank Ada Derana 24 and the Business Best team for the opportunity to share our perspective on AI and its evolving landscape. Watch the full interview below.
