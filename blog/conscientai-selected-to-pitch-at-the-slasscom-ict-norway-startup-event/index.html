ConscientAI selected to pitch at the SLASSCOM–ICT Norway startup event

Sep 5, 2017 · 2 min read

We are pleased to share that ConscientAI was among eight Sri Lankan startups selected to pitch at an event jointly organized by the Sri Lanka Association for Software and Services Companies (SLASSCOM) and ICT Norway on August 30. The event provided local startups with an opportunity to present their ideas to a panel of industry representatives, with the three most promising teams set to receive sponsorship from ICT Norway to attend Oslo Innovation Week in Norway from September 25 to 29.

This initiative is part of a broader collaboration between SLASSCOM and ICT Norway to support the growth of Sri Lanka’s technology sector and create opportunities for local startups to connect with the international innovation ecosystem. Through this partnership, the two organizations are working to encourage entrepreneurship, promote technology‑driven solutions, and strengthen ties between the Sri Lankan and Norwegian technology industries.

At the pitching event, we presented our idea for an intelligent fashion assistant that uses deep learning and computer vision to analyze images and extract fashion‑related attributes, such as clothing types and colors. We also demonstrated a working deep learning and computer vision pipeline that identifies and extracts these attributes from visual data. This work represents one of our efforts to explore practical applications of artificial intelligence and develop intelligent systems that can address real‑world needs.

We are grateful to SLASSCOM and ICT Norway for creating this opportunity and for bringing together emerging startups to present their ideas. It was a valuable experience for our team to share our work with the industry panel and take part in an initiative that encourages innovation and supports the growth of Sri Lanka’s technology ecosystem.
